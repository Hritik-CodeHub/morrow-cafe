# AI Usage

## Tools Used

- ChatGPT

## What I Used AI For

I used AI during development to brainstorm UI structure,
review component organization, and identify accessibility
and edge cases.

## One Useful Thing AI Helped With

AI helped identify accessibility details such as
aria-live status messaging, aria-invalid form states,
keyboard navigation and reduced-motion support.

## One Thing AI Got Wrong Or That I Changed

Some generated suggestions were more complex than necessary
for the scope of the assessment. I simplified the architecture
and avoided unnecessary dependencies.

## What I Personally Reviewed

I personally reviewed the component structure, validation
logic, API state handling, responsive behavior, accessibility,
visual design and final implementation.
